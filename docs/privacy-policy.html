Nodoro – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026 · App: Nodoro (app.kohese.nothingtimer) · Developer: Kaivalya Beohar

Nodoro is a Pomodoro timer for Android with Nothing Phone Glyph support. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: your timer data stays on your device, and the Google Play version shows ads and offers an optional purchase, which involve Google services described below.

1. Information stored on your device

Nodoro stores the following only on your device: timer durations, alarm, theme and Glyph settings, daily and lifetime focus statistics, onboarding state, and (Google Play version) your Pro purchase status and rewarded-pass counters. The developer cannot access this data. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage, and the in-app “Reset stats” option deletes your statistics at any time.

2. Information we collect

We do not run any server, require no account, and do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or files. We do not sell personal data.

3. Third-party services (Google Play version only)

  • Google AdMob shows banner/native, interstitial and rewarded ads to users who have not unlocked Pro. AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform asks for your consent where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland). You can change your choice where the consent form offers it, and reset your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Google Play Billing processes the optional one-time “Nodoro Pro” purchase. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; we only learn whether the purchase is valid.

The GitHub (open) version of Nodoro contains none of these services, does not request the Internet permission and makes no network connections.

4. Permissions

  • VIBRATE – haptic feedback and alarm vibration.
  • com.nothing.ketchum.permission.ENABLE – drives the Glyph lights on supported Nothing phones.
  • Google Play version only: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, AD_ID and billing permissions, used by ads and purchases.

5. Children

Nodoro is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data retention and your rights

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data held by Google is governed by Google’s policies and controls. Depending on your location you may have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection; contact us if you need help.

7. Security

Local data is kept in the app’s private storage. The rewarded-pass counters are signed with a key in the Android Keystore to detect tampering.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
